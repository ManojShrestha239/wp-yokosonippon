Documentation

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Counseling

Initial meeting to discuss goals and select suitable institutions.

Step 2: Language Training

Enroll in Japanese courses to meet eligibility.

Step 3: Document Collection

Gather all academic and financial records for submission.

Step 4: COE Application

Submit application to Japan Immigration for Certificate of Eligibility.

Step 5: Tuition Payment

Upon COE approval, pay the required tuition to the institution.
